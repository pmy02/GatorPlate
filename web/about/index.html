How refriGator works

refriGator is a student-built prototype that helps SF State students check CalFresh, California's food benefit, in one short conversation. It is not an official SF State, county, or CalFresh service.

What refriGator is

A student talks with refriGator by phone in English, or in the browser in English or Spanish, by voice or by typing. refriGator asks only the questions that can change the answer. The student leaves with an estimate and a card with the next steps: answers for the BenefitsCal application, the documents to gather and what the county interview will ask.

On the phone, a hosted voice gateway turns speech into text and sends only the text to refriGator. In the browser, the browser's own speech recognition does the same, and typing always works.

The model listens, rules decide, a person confirms

  1. The model listens

    A language model turns what the student says into answers, such as units, rent and income. It never writes a sentence and never sets a number.

  2. Rules decide

    A dated rules table computes the estimate, and every sentence comes from a sentence bank written in advance. A question is asked only when its answer can change the result; the coordinator sees why each question was asked and which ones were skipped.

  3. A person confirms

    Anything uncertain becomes a yellow line. The SF State CalFresh coordinator confirms or edits each one before a case can be marked reviewed. The county makes every eligibility decision.

When the estimate is likely, the card also lists other help CalFresh can open this year, such as Medi-Cal coverage, Clipper START, California LifeLine and tax credits. Those amounts come from a second dated table and are computed by code, never by the AI. Each agency decides. Not a promise.

What is kept

refriGator keeps the student's answers and short quotes of their words, not the conversation.

  • No audio
  • No Social Security numbers
  • No immigration status
  • No phone numbers

Digits that look like a Social Security or card number are removed before anything else sees the text. Students can delete their information from their card.

Why SF State

refriGator is built for two groups: SF State students, who get a fast, private estimate and one thing to do today, and the SF State CalFresh coordinator, who gets cases that arrive checked, with what the student said, what was assumed and why.

Limits

  • An estimate only. The county makes every eligibility decision.
  • English on the phone; English and Spanish on the web.
  • Not connected to county or school systems. refriGator never applies, signs or submits anything for a student.
  • The Spanish text is waiting for a review by a native speaker.
  • Contacts and hours were checked on October 1, 2026 and can change.
